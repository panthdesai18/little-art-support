# Artwork credits

Little Art 0.3 contains 40 historic artworks: 38 from the Art Institute of Chicago and two from the Cleveland Museum of Art. Museum records identify the artworks as public domain; reproduction licenses are listed individually below.

Two-sentence notes are adapted for Little Art from Art Institute of Chicago descriptions under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and Cleveland CC0 collection information. The Poet’s Garden note is original visual commentary. Titles may be shortened; movement labels are editorial. Museums do not endorse Little Art.
