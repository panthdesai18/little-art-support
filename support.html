Little Art for Mac

Little Art support

Change wallpaper

Choose a painting, then click Bring it to my desktop in version 0.3, or Set as wallpaper in version 0.4 and later. In version 0.3, Make it yours contains full-painting framing. Version 0.4 adds a desktop preview and Fit/Fill controls beside the painting: Fit preserves the full composition with borders, while Fill crops the edges to cover the screen.

Daily changes

Turn on Change daily and keep Little Art running. A manual wallpaper choice lasts for the rest of that day. Launch at login is optional in Make it yours (version 0.3) or Settings (version 0.4 and later).

Desktop card and widgets

Desktop art card shows the last wallpaper applied by Little Art. The native Daily Art widget shows the day’s collection selection independently. Add it through macOS Edit Widgets and search for Little Art; medium and large include the two-sentence story.

Multiple desktops

Wallpaper changes target the active desktop on connected screens. Other Spaces and lock screens may use different macOS settings.

Offline use

All 40 artworks and stories are included. Only external museum and licensing links need an internet connection.
