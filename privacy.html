Little Art privacy policy

Little Art does not collect or transmit personal data from the app. It has no user accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Your favorites, wallpaper selection, framing preference, and daily rotation preference are stored locally on your Mac. Wallpaper image copies are stored in the app’s local container. The app accesses connected-display information to apply wallpaper. Its artwork collection and stories are bundled for offline use.

Optional launch-at-login registration is managed by macOS. The Daily Art widget uses local artwork and a local schedule.

Museum and licensing links open external websites in your browser. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies. Apple may separately process purchases, diagnostics, or App Store information under your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

If you contact support, the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or payment details.
